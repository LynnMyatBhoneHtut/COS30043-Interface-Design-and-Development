About Us

We source green beans directly from smallholder farms across Southeast Asia and roast them in small batches every Tuesday and Friday. Nothing leaves the roastery more than five days after it comes out of the drum.

Visit our tasting bar in Damansara Uptown, or order online and have your beans delivered anywhere in Malaysia.
